BAKERY v1.0.0 - koobacorp drum synth
macOS 11 or newer - Apple Silicon + Intel (universal) - VST3 + AU instrument

INSTALL (when you decide to)
1. Close FL Studio.
2. Open Terminal, drag install_mac.sh from this folder into it, press Return.
   It asks for your Mac password (the system plug-in folders need it), asks
   before replacing an old BAKERY, and moves any old copy to your Desktop.
   It installs:
     BAKERY.vst3      -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     BAKERY.component -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   Manual alternative: copy the two bundles into those folders in Finder
   (Finder asks for your password).
3. FL Studio > Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
   BAKERY appears under Generators (manufacturer koobacorp).

The bundles are ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized. The helper removes the
download quarantine flag for these two bundles only. Never disable macOS
security globally.

FIRST SOUND
Add BAKERY to the Channel rack, press Play in FL: the 909 House kit groove plays
(the sequencer follows FL's transport and tempo). Or play MIDI notes:
C3 kick (MIDI 36), D3 snare (38), F#3 closed hat (42), A#3 open hat (46).
Full note map, separate outputs and a beginner guide: README.md and LEARN.md.

SEPARATE MIXER TRACKS IN FL
In BAKERY's KIT MIXER set a channel's Output to "Out 2".."Out 13".
In FL: route BAKERY to a free mixer track (Ctrl+L in the Channel rack), then
plugin gear (wrapper settings) > Processing tab > Connections > Auto map outputs.
Out 2 lands on the next mixer track, Out 3 on the one after. Details: README.md.
